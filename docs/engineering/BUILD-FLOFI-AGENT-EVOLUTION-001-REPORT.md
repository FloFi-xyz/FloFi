# BUILD-FLOFI-AGENT-EVOLUTION-001

Audited the 19 existing FloFi engineering domains at `b84dd67721aa085a38b1621893abcfaefd7a5867`, the fetched `origin/main` on 2026-11-18. The baseline engineering guard found 176 modules and no ownership drift. The audit covered the repository instructions, both role sets and their loader configuration, Claude hooks and local verification harnesses, coverage and skills inventories, and CircleCI governance and certification. For product claims, the audit compared registry rows, actual runtime gates and regression assertions with ADRs, merged PRs and incident reports.

This change updates engineering roles, knowledge, coverage mappings and advisory tooling. No product runtime, production configuration, credentials, schema, wallet authorization behavior, deployment or financial operation changed. Codex definitions are canonical and Claude definitions are generated from them. Six independent specialist reviews covered the technical boundaries in batches of at most three. Main combined the findings and assessed every role. These reviews are advisory.

## Reusable lessons and stale knowledge

- **Authorization, fees and recovery.** A lagging wallet nonce warrants a bounded reread. A mismatched or unavailable nonce closes the attempt. Reconcile unknown submissions with the appropriate receipt proof before creating new authority. A relay may complete without consuming the owner's nonce. A changed payer or material payload change requires new Review. The execution and EVM skills record these rules, and the swap, wallet, bridge and workflow specialists point to their contracts. [Nonce conflict and support controls](https://github.com/FloFi-xyz/FloFi/pull/196), [Mainnet reliability](../builds/BUILD-P0-MAINNET-EXECUTION-RELIABILITY.md)
- **Identity and continuity.** Exact chain/asset slots outlive short follow-ups, and native and wrapped assets remain distinct. Preserve current conversation, Review and reconciled prefix across panel changes, reload and expiry. Bound the quote window immediately before the next handoff. Preserve explicit fresh provider proof through Google login and account linking. [Beta regressions](../builds/BUILD-FLOFI-BETA-REGRESSION-FIX-001.md), [Google P0 incident](../builds/BUILD-FLOFI-AUTH-UX-LOGIN-REGRESSION-FIX-GOOGLE-P0.md), [Execution UX reliability](../builds/BUILD-FLOFI-EXECUTION-UX-RELIABILITY-001.md)
- **Provider and client failures.** Preserve independent provider results and deadlines. Late comparison quotes confer no execution authority. Keep the exact grammar fallback and label incomplete discovery. External MCP clients require their own acceptance evidence even when OAuth metadata and error responses are healthy. [MCP link incident](../builds/BUILD-MCP-LINK-INTEGRITY-P0.md), [Router incident report](../builds/HOTFIX-UNIVERSAL-BRIDGE-P0/REPORT.md)
- **Release and CI.** A successful transpiled test cannot prove that native Node accepts lazy imports. Schema certificate fixtures and deployed schemas require distinct evidence. A stale copy assertion or missing server-action stub is deterministic; only a demonstrated transient warrants the existing same-SHA retry. [Replica drain and shutdown](https://github.com/FloFi-xyz/FloFi/pull/205), [CI lifecycle](CI.md), [Schema compatibility](../deploy/SCHEMA-COMPATIBILITY.md)

The active guide now points to current evidence instead of a draft PR #137 follow-up from the prior build. The swap role drops a historical concurrent-P0 instruction. The EVM skill scopes type-2 signing to the managed direct path. The inventory replaces dated Turnkey and telemetry conclusions with current evidence gaps. Five existing tests now have representative links for Base catalog, mainnet policy, Arbitrum/chat parity and Aave reserves. Existing unconditional suites retain their status.
