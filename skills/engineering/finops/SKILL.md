# FinOps

- Separate organization/project billing data from model estimates. The V2 attempt meter tracks eligible live calls and tool rounds, including failures, and uses the returned model and available token categories. A provider success can incur cost even when intent validation fails. Use exact dated model rates and complete usage samples for estimates. Cache reads, cache writes and reasoning have provider-specific semantics. Missing usage is unknown. Preserve cost per logical request versus provider attempt and avoid double-counting an estimate alongside its bill. Direct project-scoped OpenAI Costs import is conditional. Other provider costs use the reviewed manual/CSV ledger contract. [Attempt aggregation](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/admin/finops/ai.ts) · [Provider importer](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/admin/finops/providers.ts)
- Keep Vercel usage, Railway subscription and resource usage, and Neon compute/storage/transfer in their distinct scopes. Check current official meter definitions, billing plans, periods, environments, currency and shared versus direct attribution. Allocate shared infrastructure with an explicit driver and label the allocation. Cost per user or workflow needs a valid aligned denominator and separation between modeled scenarios and observed aggregates. The current attempt meter has no per-user ledger. [Economic scopes and calculations](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/admin/finops/economics.ts) · [Scenario model](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/admin/finops/scenario.ts)
- Validate scope/environment, currency, dates, provenance, coverage, overlap, import deduplication and explicit zero versus missing rows. Follow invoice/accounting over provider cost precedence within their scope; usage and assumption records are reported separately and never count toward recorded expenses, so a zero-spend required scope is complete only with an invoice record. Reconcile realized fee receipts separately from unsettled obligations or projections. Report contribution and infrastructure-adjusted operating result with their cost coverage. The canonical workflow cohort excludes legacy/single flows and lacks direct workflow costs. Cross-channel distinct active users are unavailable. Compare budgets and anomalies by scope, source and period, including failed-attempt spend and late invoice adjustments. [CSV validation](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/admin/finops/csv.ts) · [Report](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/admin/finops/report.ts) · [Ledger regression](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/admin/finops/finops.pg.test.ts)
- Treat credentials, private invoices and production records as operator-controlled. Use fixture data for review. FinOps schema activation follows its own owner-run compatibility contract. [Neon and PostgreSQL](../neon-postgresql/SKILL.md)
