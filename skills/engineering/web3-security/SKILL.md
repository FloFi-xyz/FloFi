# Web3 security

- Trace the exact authorized payload from canonical workflow and Review through account, wallet, chain, adapter, recipient, asset, allowance/spender, calldata or instructions, fee payer, ceilings and expiry. Resolve contracts and tokens from canonical identity and independent chain reads. Decode and validate provider data and reject malicious or inconsistent token metadata, targets, signers and outputs. Bind signatures to protocol-specific domains and nonces. Refuse chain/tenant mismatches and stale grants even when an upstream RPC, relayer or AI host claims success. [Semantic invalidation](../../../packages/workflow-contracts/src/invalidation.ts) · [Execution skill](../../operations/execution/SKILL.md)
- Check durable idempotency at the wallet request, submission and settlement boundaries. Classify failed, rejected and unknown states separately. After a nonce conflict, use bounded rereads and path-specific reconciliation to determine whether authority was consumed before allowing a new attempt. A wallet relay can settle without consuming an EOA nonce. Preserve verified prefix steps through reload and check delivery before dependent spending. [Nonce regression](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/domain/wallet-nonce.test.ts) · [Settlement regression](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/state/settlement-sync.test.ts) · [Nonce incident follow-up](https://github.com/FloFi-xyz/FloFi/pull/196)
- Verify session freshness, project/tenant scopes and wallet binding on every API, approval and recovery entry point. Linking and duplicate recovery require explicit fresh proof and the allowed disposition. Treat prompts, tool metadata and partner responses as untrusted inputs. Keep credentials and private approval capabilities out of logs, URLs shown in chat and reports. [Account recovery regression](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/accounts/account-service.pg.test.ts) · [MCP approval link regression](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/mcp/link-integrity.pg.test.ts) · [Identity recovery follow-up](https://github.com/FloFi-xyz/FloFi/pull/208)
- Preserve pinned dependencies, audits and supply-chain checks. Validate provider hosts and sanitize incident evidence. Exercise wrong tenant, wallet, chain, asset, target, payer, expired authorization, replay/duplicate protection and bounded reconciliation using existing fixtures and isolated test paths. Capture the concrete failure and a regression tied to the governing boundary.
