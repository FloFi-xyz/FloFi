# Robinhood Chain

- The Nitro EVM network uses native ETH (18 decimals). The mainnet pin is `eip155:4663` (`0x1237`), with `rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com`. The engineering testnet pin is `eip155:46630` (`0xb626`), with `rpc.testnet.chain.robinhood.com`. Use the chain-bound, method-limited RPC and verify `eth_chainId`, canonical deployments and pinned quote blocks. Treat rate limits, incorrect chains, missing code and failed simulation as distinct failures. The liquidity path needs `eth_simulateV1`. Use the action's own gas estimate and reviewed cap, including applicable Nitro posting costs in its estimate. An OP Stack L1 fee formula does not apply here. [Network and deployment pins](../../../packages/action-registry/src/robinhood-chain.ts) · [RPC and runtime gates](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/server/robinhood-mainnet.ts)
- Resolve native ETH, canonical WETH (18) and USDG (6) by chain, contract and decimals. USDG on Robinhood and USDC on Base/Arbitrum are distinct assets. Stock Tokens require the active registry entry, token identity, verified proxy/beacon and the current and scheduled shares-per-token multiplier at the quote block. Transfers use raw token units. The multiplier is WAD-scaled and may affect quoted economic value. FloFi's generic portfolio feeds do not price these Stock Tokens or USDG. Refuse unverified metadata or a material multiplier change in the review window. [Asset registry](../../../packages/action-registry/src/asset-registry.ts) · [Stock Token metadata](../../../packages/action-registry/src/robinhood-stock-tokens.ts) · [Swap validation](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/server/public-testnet-service.ts)
- For provider pricing research, Robinhood documents a raw underlying-equity REST bid/ask and a per-token oracle price that already reflects the multiplier. This is provider documentation with pending licence verification and establishes no additional FloFi price source. [Robinhood pricing API](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-token-apis/)
- Mainnet registers Uniswap V3 exact-input swaps and concentrated liquidity, WETH9 conversion, native/catalog ERC-20 transfers and two bridge profiles. The finite router verifies specific USDG↔USDC and WETH↔WETH pairs. The universal router verifies Relay Depository paths. Reads, authoring, Review and owner execution have separate conditions, including deployment opt-ins. The eligible 0x gasless branch is covered in the [0x skill](../../protocols/0x/SKILL.md). An advertised venue, contract address or provider quote establishes neither FloFi eligibility nor execution evidence. Testnet permits only the capped native owner self-transfer. Testnet DeFi and Stock Tokens and Robinhood Aave execution have no supported profile. [Capability rows](../../../packages/action-registry/src/execution-capabilities.ts) · [Finite pairs](../../../packages/action-registry/src/crosschain-router.ts)
- Check the chain and stock metadata gates, wrong wallet/chain, quote expiry, and exact transfer, swap, liquidity and bridge bindings. The `robinhood-{swap,liquidity,transfer-mainnet}.test.ts`, `router-robinhood.test.ts` and `robinhood-composition.test.ts` suites use controlled evidence. A bridge→buy→transfer workflow preserves separate settlement and Review boundaries. The registered Robinhood mainnet rows carry no evidence ceiling.
