# Gas abstraction

- Mechanism selection is per execution step or eligible execution group. Keep native wallet gas when the balance covers the bound or coverage is unknown. A confirmed shortage can select a deployment-enabled and proven eligible alternative for that exact chain, action and wallet. Otherwise preserve the native shortage and precise reason for alternative failure. Keep terminal provider faults visible. [Mechanism policy](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/gas-abstraction/mechanism.ts) · [Provider configuration](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/gas-abstraction/config.ts)
- **Circle Paymaster.** Eligible Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum groups use a pinned ERC-4337 EntryPoint and owner USDC with an EIP-2612 permit capped to reviewed authorization. Account and version eligibility and first-use upgrade consent are covered by [account abstraction](../account-abstraction/SKILL.md).
- **Pimlico Kora.** The configured Solana mainnet fee payer can front SOL network fees and applicable account rent for supported token transfers, Jupiter swaps and eligible sign-all groups. Direct SOL transfers, Devnet and universal bridge are outside this path. Pin the payer in message key 0, payment wallet and exact USDC/USDT repayment instruction in the owner-reviewed message. Verify payer signatures and byte identity. Recover an ambiguous result before issuing a different attempt. A bounded identical-byte path is provider-specific. [Kora runtime](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/gas-abstraction/kora/runtime.ts) · [Transfer regression](../../../apps/reference-dapp/src/server/solana-transfer-kora.test.ts)
- **0x Gasless.** The relayer fronts gas for eligible Robinhood ERC-20 swaps and charges costs from the trade. Follow the [0x contract](../../protocols/0x/SKILL.md) for allowance, fee and reconciliation checks. **Operator sponsorship** is a separate, off-by-default path with deployment allowlists and spending controls. The owner-paid policy never selects it. EVM uses the vetted EIP-5792 wallet batch route. The Solana operator-sponsorship helper is unwired. [Sponsorship runbook](../../../docs/deploy/GAS-SPONSORSHIP.md)
- Bind payer, fee currency, base-unit ceilings, fee components, rent, balances and wallet prompts to Review. Recheck at authorization. Changed payer, material economics or changed payload require applicable fresh Review. Verify both owner debit and fee attribution in reconciliation. Exercise native-first and unknown-native cases, rejected eligibility, pinned provider failures, caps and changed-mechanism refusals.
